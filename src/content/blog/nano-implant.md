A landmark clinical trial has successfully restored tactile perception to a patient with chronic nerve damage using a nano-implant.

The device works by:
1. Intercepting surface signals via micro-sensors.
2. Translating signals into neural impulses.
3. Beaming impulses to the somatosensory cortex.

"The warmth of a cup of coffee... it's a miracle," the patient remarked.
