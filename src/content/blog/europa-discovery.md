The IEA has released high-resolution images showing "chaos terrains" on Jupiter's moon Europa. These regions suggest active hydrothermal activity.

The mission detected:
- **Thermal anomalies** in thin ice regions.
- **Nutrient exchange** potential with the surface.
- **Liquid water reservoirs** just kilometers deep.

"These are prime spots in our search for life," said Dr. Anita Vance.
