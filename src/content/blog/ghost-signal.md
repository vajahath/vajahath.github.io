In this experiment, we are verifying the layout integrity of our news portal when a story lacks a `heroImage`. 

A robust design system should handle empty states or missing data gracefully without breaking the visual rhythm of the grid. In a high-density environment like ours, the absence of an image can actually serve as a powerful "Focus" element, drawing the eye directly to the typography and the core of the message.

### Why go text-only?
1. **Speed**: Instant load times for critical information.
2. **Focus**: Zero distractions from the core journalistic take.
3. **Accessibility**: Pure semantic structure remains the gold standard.

If you are reading this on the homepage, note how the `NewsItem` and `Focus` components adapt to the missing hero asset.
