Central banks are accelerating plans for sovereign digital currencies (CBDCs) as DeFi platforms capture more global trade.

Key developments include:
- **Instant Settlements** for cross-border trade.
- **Enhanced Security** through cryptographic verification.
- **Economic Sovereignty** in a fragmented landscape.

"We are witnessing the birth of a new financial architecture," says Elena Rodrigues.
