Explorers aboard the vessel *Polaris* have reported a groundbreaking discovery in the Arctic Ocean. Advanced AUVs found a bioluminescent ecosystem thousands of meters beneath the ice.

The habitat is powered by hydrothermal vents, supporting:
- **Translucent crustaceans** with unique shielding.
- **Chemosynthetic worms** with extended lifespans.

"This changes our understanding of biological limits," noted Dr. Marcus Thorne.
